Hi, I'm Siddartha K — a passionate traveller and storyteller from Chitradurga, Karnataka. Through Sid's Destination, I document my journeys across India's most beautiful landscapes, cultural festivals, and hidden local wonders.

From the misty hills of Ooty to river rafting in Dandeli, the roaring waters of Jog Falls to the vibrant festivals of Chitradurga — every video is a new adventure waiting for you.
